# Mobile Architecture

## Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Flutter 3.38.7 (Dart 3.10.7), targeting Android APK (with Chrome web preview support).
- **State & Routing:** `flutter_riverpod` (providers & view models) + `go_router` (declarative routes with auth/role redirects).
- **Networking & Realtime:** `dio` (Bearer JWT interceptor, `X-Server-Time` header sync, idempotent `GET` cold-start retry), `socket_io_client` (Socket.IO v4).
- **Persistence & Security:** `flutter_secure_storage` (JWT & session), `shared_preferences` (theme/locale/textScale/onboarding), `hive_flutter` (offline MTOP pass & wizard drafts), `local_auth` (optional biometric unlock).
- **UI & Assets:** `google_fonts` (`Manrope` & `JetBrains Mono`), `pinput` (OTP), `qr_flutter` (Offline Digital Operator ID QR), `mobile_scanner` (Android live QR verifier behind `!kIsWeb` conditional import), `url_launcher` (web portal redirect / hotlines).

## Layered Layout (`lib/`)
- `lib/core/` — `config/`, `constants/` (25 Barangays, 3 Zones, 17 TODAs), `l10n/` (EN/FIL), `theme/` (Civic Ledger Light & Dark), `network/` (`ApiClient`, `ServerTimeSync`, `SocketIoRealtimeClient`), `services/` (`DocumentCaptureService` with conditional Android ML Kit scanner + Camera/Gallery/PDF/Roster Excel/CSV picker), `storage/` (SecureSession, SharedPreferences, Biometric, Hive `OfflineWalletCache`, Hive `WizardDraftCache`), `utils/` (`ManilaDateFormatter`).
- `lib/data/` — `models/` (User + `PortalAnnouncement`, AuthResponse, PortalSettings, RosterCheckResult, `FranchiseRecord`, `ViolationHistoryResponse`, `FranchiseWizardDraft`, `OcrScanResult`, `toda_models`, `communication_models`), `repositories/` (AuthRepository + profile/recovery/password, SettingsRepository, `FranchiseRepository`, `TodaRepository`, `CommunicationRepository`).
- `lib/shared/widgets/` — `CivicButton`, `CivicCard`, `CivicTextField`, `CivicStatusBadge`, `LguAdvisorySheet`, `RoleGuardDialog`.
- `lib/features/` — `splash/`, `onboarding/`, `auth/`, `shell/` (Role-Adaptive 4-tab Operator / 5-tab TODA shell with live notification & chat badges + `session_revoked` redirect), `garage/` (Operator Garage Dashboard, 5-Stage LGU Pipeline Stepper, Unit Specs, Violations History, Withdraw & Voluntary Cancellation sheets), `wallet/` (3D-Flip Digital Operator ID Card, Watermarked MTOP E-Copy & Payment E-Receipt `OR#`, Offline Digital Wallet sheet), `application/` (5-Step Apply & Fix-Reapply Wizard, Renewal Wizard, Smart Document Scanner + 3-Tier AI OCR cards), `toda/` (TODA President Governance Hub, Member Directory & 4 KPIs, Individual Add + Auto-Next Body#, AI OCR Roster Editor & OVM Submissions Tracker, Disaffiliation Removal Requests, Web-Safe Built-in QR Verifier), `updates/` (Socket.IO Live Support Chat with Tagalog FAQ chips & typing indicator, Official LGU Broadcast & Portal Advisories, Real-Time Notifications Center), `settings/` (Profile & Photo Edit, OTP Account Recovery, Change Password + JWT/Socket rotation, Operator Document Vault, Helpdesk Tickets & Gasan LGU Hotlines, Display/Accessibility, Offline Cache Management).
